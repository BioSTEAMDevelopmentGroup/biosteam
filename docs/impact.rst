Scientific Papers
-----------------

BioSTEAM has enabled over 55 published studies. A majority of studies have 
leveraged the BioSTEAM platform to compare conversion technologies, 
inform policy making, and build new modeling tools. Modeled technologies 
include a range of fermentation-based bioproduct pathways, going from 
conventional/cellulosic crops, municipal solid waste, and flue gas all
the way to diols, organic acids, oleochemicals, and biofuels. Newer applications
for BioSTEAM include thermochemical upcycling of waste plastics through
pyrolysis and solvent-based dissolution and precipitation.

Software Tools & AI
-------------------

to build new tools, some of which have AI-integration. Tools with AI integration 
are still in early stages of development. Here are a few of the projects which 
have been openly shared:
